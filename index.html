About

Engineering iOS apps
that scale & delight

With over 8 years in iOS development, I have grown from writing my first Swift lines to owning end-to-end product delivery as a senior developer and team lead, consistently shipping high-quality applications that users love.

I specialise in Swift, SwiftUI, and UIKit with deep expertise in clean architecture patterns (MVVM, MVP, VIPER), modern concurrency (async/await, GCD), and performance optimisation using ARC and Instruments. My apps span healthcare, transport, media, and property management industries.

At Nexios Technologies, I own the full iOS lifecycle from requirement analysis and architecture design to CI/CD pipelines with Xcode Cloud and Fastlane, App Store submission, and post-release monitoring via Crashlytics and Sentry.
